        Straight answers
        
      
        Questions?
        Asked & answered.
        
      
        
        
        What does Sosick sell itself?
        Our own thrift shoes and clothes, picked one by one, and beads we make by hand. Each piece shows with its photo and price, or "Ask for price". See them in Categories.
        
        
        What can I order?
        Our own pieces, anything from a Verified Plug, or anything you can buy or collect in Nairobi: clothes and shoes from a shop or market stall, a parcel from a bus office, or something from one of our Verified Plugs. Not sure? Ask on WhatsApp.
        
        
        How much is delivery?
        You pay the item's price plus a delivery fee. We tell you the exact fee on WhatsApp before the rider leaves, so there are no surprises.
        
        
        When do I pay, and how?
        When it arrives. Check it first, then pay with M-Pesa or cash.
        
        
        Do I need an app or an account?
        No. Fill the order slip and it opens WhatsApp with everything typed out. Or just text 0112 275 881.
        
        
        Do you deliver to my area?
        Check the route lists above. Not on them? Order anyway and we'll tell you honestly if a rider can reach you.
        
        
        What is a Verified Plug?
        A seller who passed all five checks of the Sosick Test: we bought from them ourselves first. If they slip, they lose the badge. See the plugs
        
        
        How do I follow my order?
        Open Track and type your order code (like SOS-1234) and the last 4 digits of the phone you ordered with.
        
        
        What do you do with my details?
        We only use them to deliver your order. Read how we handle your details.

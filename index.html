Stop 3Verified Plugs

Sellers we've bought from ourselves.

A Verified Plug is a seller outside Sosick, like an online thrift page or a market stall, that we tested by secretly buying from them first. They only get a stall here if every check passes.

  1. We buy in secret, like any normal customer.
  2. The item matches the photos.
  3. The price is honest: no hidden costs, no changes after you pay.
  4. They're on time.
  5. They fix problems and handle returns properly.
  • ✓ VerifiedPassed all 5 checks
  • ★ Trusted3+ months, no complaints
  • ♛ Top PlugThe comrades' favourite

How you buy: pick something from a plug, tell Sosick, and we collect it from them and bring it to you. You check it before you pay. If a plug lets buyers down, they lose the badge.
